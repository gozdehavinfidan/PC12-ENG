## Gantt — moved to the dashboard

> The ASCII Gantt that used to live here has been **removed on purpose**.
>
> It was a *second copy* of the task list, and after one week the two copies
> had already drifted: `T0.3` was owned by `E` in the WBS table but `G B` in
> the Gantt, and `T2.5` was `G` in the table but `E` in the Gantt. Storing one
> fact in two places guarantees this — the only reliable fix is to store it
> once.
>
> **Live schedule → the dashboard** (`docs/index.html`, Gantt view), whose
> single source of truth is `docs/data.js`. See ADR **D7**.

## Critical path
`T1.1/1.3 (data)` → `T1.4 (preprocess)` → `T2.3/2.5 (train)` → `T3.1/3.2
(features)` → `T3.4 (eval)` → `T5.3 (wire app)`. **Data is the gate** — if
labeling slips, everything downstream slips. Mitigations in the Risk register.

## Risk register (top)
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Dataset small / poorly labeled / format unknown | **HIGH (n≈70 confirmed)** | High | `15-SMALL-DATA-STRATEGY` — 5-fold CV split **by image**, pretrained encoder + patching, Cellpose-SAM zero-shot baseline, augmentation as regularisation, batch-2 held-out test |
| ~~No GPU / slow training~~ — **resolved, D12** | — | — | A5000 24GB + A6000 48GB: 5-fold CV, pretrained backbones, foundation-model fine-tune all feasible; A6000 for long overnight jobs, A5000 for dev/iterative runs |
| Overlapping neurites → instance separation fails | High | High | Multi-class seg (cell+neurite) first; instance only where resolvable; report limitation |
| Neurite **angle** is ill-defined without a reference | Med | Med | Define metric precisely in T0.3 (per-neurite vs dominant direction) |
| 2 people, 1 semester = tight | High | High | Milestone gates; **İP7 confirmed** (batch 2 ETA ~W3); cut scope to `[PLANNED]` core, keep rest as `[IDEA]` |
| Labeling consistency low | Med | Med | 10% double-labeling + IAA (Dice/Cohen) check in T1.6 |
