clDice · centreline Dice
Is every neurite found, without gaps or invented branches?
centreline = 1-pixel skeleton of a mask
Tprec = predicted centreline inside the label
Tsens = label centreline inside the prediction
clDice = 2 · Tprec · Tsens / (Tprec + Tsens)
Example: one branch missed → Tprec = 1.00 · Tsens ≈ 0.62 → clDice ≈ 0.76
1 = every neurite complete · gaps and invented branches lower it

Dice · pixel overlap
How much of the cell-body area is right?
Dice = 2 · |P ∩ L| / (|P| + |L|)
P = predicted cell-body pixels · L = labelled cell-body pixels
Example: 90 pixels shared, 100 predicted, 100 labelled → 2·90 / 200 = 0.90
1 = perfect overlap · two touching cells drawn as one blob still score high
Object F1 · cell-by-cell matching
Is every cell found as its own object?
a predicted cell matches a labelled cell if IoU ≥ 0.5 · each cell matched at most once
IoU = overlap / union of the two cells
precision = matched / predicted cells
recall = matched / labelled cells
F1 = 2 · precision · recall / (precision + recall)
Example: 10 labelled cells, 2 touching cells merged into 1 → 9 predicted, 8 matched
precision 0.89 · recall 0.80 → F1 ≈ 0.84
1 = every cell found once · merged or split cells lower it
Count error · cell count
How far is the number of cells from the truth?
count error = |Npredicted − Nlabelled| / Nlabelled
computed per image, then averaged over the images
Example: 10 labelled cells, 8 found → |8 − 10| / 10 = 0.20
0 = exact count · 0.21 ≈ count off by about one cell in five

Training loss
What every network on this slide minimises while it learns (nnU-Net uses its own training setup)
L = BCE + Dice + λ · soft-clDice
BCE = − mean [ y · log p + (1 − y) · log (1 − p) ]
every pixel: is it the right class?
Dice loss = 1 − 2 · Σ p·y / (Σ p + Σ y)
the whole structure: overlap, even though 97 % of the pixels are background
soft-clDice loss = 1 − 2 · Tprec · Tsens / (Tprec + Tsens)
centre lines (soft skeletons): punishes broken neurites
p = predicted probability · y = label (0 or 1) · λ = weight of soft-clDice (1.0)
soft-clDice added: clDice +0.023 · Betti-0 error 9.6 → 7.4 · junction F1 +0.031
